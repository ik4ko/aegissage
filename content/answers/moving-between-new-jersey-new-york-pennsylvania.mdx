When you move between New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania, Original Medicare goes with you unchanged. A Medicare Advantage Plan or a Part D drug plan may not, because each plan has a service area. A move out of that area gives you a Special Enrollment Period, roughly the month before you move and the 2 full months after, to choose a plan where you now live.

## What stays the same when I move with Medicare?

Part A and Part B. They work in New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania with any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare, and your Medicare card and number do not change when you move. Only private plans and state programs need a second look.

Part A and Part B work in all three states with any doctor or hospital that takes Medicare.

## What do I have to choose again after I move?

Your Medicare Advantage or drug plan. They are offered county by county, so a plan you had in Bergen County may not exist in Brooklyn or Philadelphia. After you move out of the service area, you can switch plans or go back to Original Medicare.

A plan you had in Bergen County may not be offered in Brooklyn or in Philadelphia, and the doctors in its network will be different. After a move out of the service area you can:

- switch to a new Medicare Advantage Plan or drug plan, or
- go back to Original Medicare, if you were in a Medicare Advantage Plan.

**Timing.** Tell your plan before you move and the window opens the month before the month you move. It closes 2 full months after the move either way. If you do nothing, you are placed in Original Medicare when your old Medicare Advantage Plan drops you.

## How are Medigap rules different in New Jersey, New York and Pennsylvania?

You can keep a Medigap policy you already have wherever you live, as long as you stay in Original Medicare. Buying a new one is where the states differ: New York lets you buy at any time, while New Jersey and Pennsylvania use stricter rules.

Buying a new one is where the states differ:

- **New York** requires any insurer that sells Medigap to accept a Medicare enrollee's application at any time throughout the year.
- **Pennsylvania** uses the federal rule: a one-time 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period that starts the first month you have Part B and are 65 or older.
- **New Jersey** also follows the federal 6-month period, and adds rules for people under 65 who have Medicare because of a disability.

So the direction of the move matters. See [Medigap underwriting in New Jersey and New York](/answers/medigap-underwriting-new-jersey-new-york).

## Do state Medicare help programs move with me?

No. Medicaid, Medicare Savings Programs and state prescription programs are run by each state, with each state's own rules, and you must live in the state. If you get help in the state you are leaving, apply again in the new one.

The same goes for state prescription assistance programs.

## What is the checklist for moving with Medicare?

Change your address with Social Security, because that is how Medicare's records update. Tell your plan the date of the move, before you go if you can. Then check that your doctors and pharmacies are in the network of any plan you consider.

1. Change your address with Social Security. That is how Medicare's records are updated.
2. Tell your plan the date of the move, before you go if you can.
3. Check that your doctors and pharmacies are in the network of any plan you are considering. See [how to check if a doctor is in a plan](/answers/how-to-check-if-a-doctor-is-in-a-medicare-plan).
4. Call the free counseling program in your new state: SHIP in New Jersey, HIICAP in New York, PA MEDI in Pennsylvania.
