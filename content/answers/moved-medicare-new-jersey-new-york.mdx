If you move to New Jersey or New York, Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) moves with you. A Medicare Advantage plan or a Part D drug plan may not, because those plans have service areas. A permanent move out of your plan's service area opens a Special Enrollment Period to join a plan where you now live.

## How long do I have to change plans after I move?

About two months. If you tell your plan before you move, your window starts the month before the move and lasts 2 full months after it. If you tell the plan after you move, it starts that month and lasts 2 more full months.

According to Medicare:

- If you tell your plan **before** you move, your chance to switch begins the month before the month you move and continues for 2 full months after you move.
- If you tell your plan **after** you move, it begins the month you tell the plan and continues for 2 more full months.

If you leave your Medicare Advantage plan's service area and do not join a new plan in that time, you are enrolled in Original Medicare when the old plan drops you. That can leave you without drug coverage until you join a drug plan, so do not let the window run out without deciding.

## What changes when I move across the Hudson?

Your plan choices. Plans are offered county by county, and Bergen and Hudson are different New Jersey counties, while each New York City borough is its own county. A plan or a doctor you had may not be available where you now live.

Plans are offered county by county. Bergen and Hudson are two different counties in New Jersey, and the five boroughs of New York City are five different New York counties. A plan you had in one may not exist in the other, and a doctor who was in your network may not be in a network where you now live.

Medigap rules also differ by state. New Jersey follows the federal 6-month Medigap Open Enrollment Period. New York lets people enrolled in Medicare buy a Medigap policy at any time. See [Medigap in New Jersey](/answers/medigap-new-jersey) and [Medicare help in New York City](/answers/medicare-help-new-york-city).

If you leave a Medicare Advantage plan because you moved out of its service area and you switch to Original Medicare, federal rules give you a right to buy certain Medigap policies. You have to apply no later than 63 days after the Medicare Advantage coverage ends.

## What should I do first after I move?

Update your address with Social Security, and tell your current plan the date you moved. Then list the doctors and prescriptions you want to keep with your new ZIP code, and compare plans in your new county at medicare.gov or with a broker.

1. Update your address with Social Security.
2. Tell your current plan the date you are moving.
3. Make a list of the doctors and prescriptions you want to keep, with your new ZIP code. The [appointment checklist](/medicare-checklist) is a printable version.
4. Compare the plans in your new county at medicare.gov, or call me and we will go through the list together.
