To choose a Medicare agent in Bergen or Hudson County, check that the person is licensed in New Jersey, ask how many insurance companies they are contracted with in your county, and make sure they follow Medicare's rules: no fee, no unrequested calls, no pressure, and no discussion of plans you have not agreed to discuss. You can also get help from NJ SHIP counselors, who do not sell insurance at all.

## What Medicare's rules say an agent may not do

According to Medicare, plans and their representatives, including independent agents and brokers, are not allowed to:

- Call or text you unless you are already a member of the plan or you gave them permission to contact you.
- Come to your home uninvited to sell you something.
- Ask for your bank account or credit card numbers just to give you a quote.
- Charge you an enrollment fee.
- Steer you toward a particular plan, or pressure you to join.
- Tell you about plan options you have not agreed to discuss, unless you ask and complete a separate appointment form.

That last rule is why a careful agent asks you to confirm in advance what you want to talk about. The form is called a Scope of Appointment. It protects you from being sold something you did not ask about.

## Six questions to ask before you share anything

1. **Are you licensed in New Jersey, and what is your National Producer Number?** You can check both with the state insurance department.
2. **How many insurance companies are you contracted with in my county?** No agent offers every plan. A good one tells you the number without being asked twice.
3. **Do you work with Medicare Advantage, Part D and Medigap, or only some of them?**
4. **Will you check my doctors and my prescriptions against each plan before recommending anything?**
5. **What happens after I enroll? Can I call you next year when something changes?**
6. **Will you tell me if staying where I am is the right answer?**

## Things that should make you hang up

- Anyone who says they are calling "from Medicare". Medicare does not call to sell you a plan.
- Anyone who asks for your Medicare number before you have agreed to work with them.
- Anyone who says you must decide today.

## Captive, independent, or no agent at all

A **captive agent** works for one insurance company and can show you that company's plans. An **independent broker** is contracted with several companies. Neither can show you every plan in the county; for the full list, use the plan finder at medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE.

You do not need an agent at all. **NJ SHIP** counselors explain your options and do not sell insurance: 1-800-792-8820.
