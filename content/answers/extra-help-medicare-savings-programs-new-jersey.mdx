New Jersey residents with limited income can apply for help with Medicare costs through one application called NJSave. It covers the Medicare Savings Programs, which can pay the Part B premium, and the state's own prescription programs, PAAD and Senior Gold. It also screens you for Extra Help, the federal program that lowers prescription drug costs.

## What each program does

- **Medicare Savings Programs (QMB, SLMB, QI).** State-run programs that pay the Medicare Part B premium. One of them, QMB, also helps with deductibles, coinsurance and copayments.
- **Extra Help (also called the Low Income Subsidy).** A federal program that lowers premiums, deductibles and copayments for Medicare drug coverage.
- **PAAD (Pharmaceutical Assistance to the Aged and Disabled).** A New Jersey program that lowers prescription costs for eligible residents.
- **Senior Gold.** A New Jersey prescription discount program with different income guidelines from PAAD.

People in PAAD and Senior Gold who are eligible for Medicare are expected to be enrolled in a Part D drug plan; the state programs work alongside it.

## How they connect

Medicare says you get Extra Help automatically if you have full Medicaid, get Supplemental Security Income, or get help from your state paying your Part B premium through a Medicare Savings Program. You can also apply for Extra Help and a Medicare Savings Program at the same time.

## Who is eligible

Each program has income limits, and some have limits on savings and other resources. The limits change every year, and states can be more generous than the federal minimum, so I do not list dollar amounts here. Medicare's own advice is to apply even if you think you are slightly over a limit.

Your state decides eligibility for Medicare Savings Programs. Social Security decides for Extra Help. I cannot tell you whether you will be approved; I can help you understand what each program is and where to apply.

## How to apply

1. Complete the NJSave application online, or request a paper application.
2. For help filling it out, call the NJSave line at 1-800-792-9745, your county Area Agency on Aging, or NJ SHIP at 1-800-792-8820.
3. Keep copies of any approval letters. They matter when you choose a drug plan.

## Why this matters when you choose a plan

Getting Extra Help or a Medicare Savings Program changes what you pay, and it can open a Special Enrollment Period to join or change a plan. If you are approved partway through the year, look at your coverage again. See [enrollment periods explained](/answers/medicare-enrollment-periods-explained).
