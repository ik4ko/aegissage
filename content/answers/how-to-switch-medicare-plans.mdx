You can switch Medicare Advantage Plans or Medicare drug plans during Open Enrollment, October 15 to December 7 each year, and the change starts January 1. A few other windows exist. To switch, you join the new plan. In most cases you do not cancel the old one, because it ends when the new coverage starts.

## When am I allowed to switch Medicare plans?

Anyone with Medicare can switch from October 15 to December 7, and changes start January 1. If you are in a Medicare Advantage plan, you can also switch once from January 1 to March 31. Other windows open after events like a move or new cost help.

- **Open Enrollment, October 15 to December 7.** Anyone with Medicare can join, drop or switch a Medicare Advantage Plan or a drug plan, or move between Original Medicare and Medicare Advantage. The plan must get your request by December 7.
- **January 1 to March 31, if you are already in a Medicare Advantage Plan.** You can switch to another Medicare Advantage Plan, or return to Original Medicare and join a separate drug plan. The change starts the first of the month after the plan gets your request.
- **The first 3 months, if you joined a Medicare Advantage Plan when you first got Medicare.** You can change to another plan or go back to Original Medicare.
- **A Special Enrollment Period.** These follow events in your life: you move, you lose other coverage, your plan is not renewed, you get or lose Medicaid or Extra Help. Each has its own length.

All of them, with their sources, are in the [enrollment calendar](/medicare-enrollment-calendar) and explained in [enrollment periods explained](/answers/medicare-enrollment-periods-explained).

## What should I check before I switch Medicare plans?

Check four things. Are all your drugs on the new plan's list? Are your doctors and pharmacy in its network? What will the year cost, counting premium, deductible and your drugs? And does the change affect other coverage you have, such as retiree benefits?

1. **Your drugs.** Look up each one on the new plan's list, and check for rules such as prior authorization.
2. **Your doctors and pharmacy.** Confirm they are in the new plan's network. See [how to check if a doctor is in a plan](/answers/how-to-check-if-a-doctor-is-in-a-medicare-plan).
3. **The costs for the year.** Compare the monthly premium, the deductible and what you would pay for the drugs you take.
4. **Other coverage.** If you have employer, union or retiree coverage, talk to the benefits administrator first. Joining a Medicare plan can end it.

Your current plan's Annual Notice of Change, which arrives in September, tells you what is changing in the plan you already have. Start there.

## How do I actually switch Medicare plans?

Join the new plan in one of three ways: at Medicare.gov, by contacting the plan, or by calling 1-800-MEDICARE. Joining the new plan usually ends the old one automatically. The new plan sends you a letter with your start date, so keep it.

## What if I have Medigap and want to switch?

Be careful in both directions. Moving from Medigap to Medicare Advantage can mean you cannot get the Medigap policy back later, and moving the other way can mean health questions. Read the details below before you drop any policy or join a new plan.

Be careful in both directions.

- **Leaving Medigap for a Medicare Advantage Plan.** In most cases you cannot get the policy back later. The first time you do this you have a 12-month trial period to return.
- **Leaving a Medicare Advantage Plan for Original Medicare.** You may want a Medigap policy, and outside your Medigap Open Enrollment Period or a guaranteed issue right a company can use medical underwriting. The rules depend on your state.
