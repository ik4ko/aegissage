In New Jersey you can buy any Medigap policy sold in the state, regardless of your health, during your Medigap Open Enrollment Period: the 6 months that begin the first month you are 65 or older and have Medicare Part B. That window happens once. After it, an insurance company can turn you down or charge more unless you have a guaranteed issue right.

## What is Medigap?

Medigap, also called Medicare Supplement Insurance, is a private policy that helps pay costs Original Medicare leaves to you, such as deductibles and coinsurance. It works only with Original Medicare, not with Medicare Advantage. Policies are standardized and named by letter.

It works only with Original Medicare. You cannot use it with a Medicare Advantage plan.

Two policies with the same letter cover the same things; the company and the price are what differ.

## When is the 6-month Medigap window in New Jersey?

It starts when two things are true: you are 65 or older, and your Part B has started. If you delay Part B because of coverage from a current job, the window waits too. If you take Part B and wait 6 months, the window is gone.

Under federal law the window starts when two things are true: you are 65 or older, and your Part B has started.

This is why the order of decisions matters when you are [turning 65 in New Jersey](/answers/turning-65-new-jersey) or [leaving employer coverage](/answers/leaving-employer-coverage-after-65).

## Can I buy Medigap after my 6-month window closes?

Sometimes. Federal rules give you a guaranteed issue right to buy certain Medigap policies in specific situations, such as when your Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare or your area, or you move out of its service area. The situations are listed below.

Federal rules give you a right to buy certain Medigap policies outside the window in specific situations. Two common ones:

- Your Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare, stops serving your area, or you move out of its service area, and you switch to Original Medicare.
- You joined a Medicare Advantage plan when you were first eligible at 65 and decide within the first year to switch to Original Medicare.

These rights have deadlines. In the first case you can apply as early as 60 days before your coverage ends and no later than 63 days after it ends.

## Can I get Medigap in New Jersey if I am under 65?

Often yes. Federal law does not guarantee it, but New Jersey does more. Insurers that sell Medigap to people 65 and older must also offer a plan to residents aged 50 to 64 who have Medicare because of a disability. Details are below.

New Jersey does more:

- **Ages 50 to 64.** Insurers that sell Medicare Supplement plans to people 65 and older must also offer a plan to New Jersey residents who become eligible for Medicare because of a disability in that age range. For people who became eligible on or after January 1, 2020, that is Medicare Supplement Plan D, and you must apply within 12 months of enrolling in Part B. (Medicare Supplement Plan D is a Medigap policy. It is not the same thing as Medicare Part D drug coverage.)
- **Under 50.** The state runs a separate program through a contracted carrier, again with a 12-month application period after Part B enrollment.

The New Jersey Department of Banking and Insurance page in the sources below has the details.
