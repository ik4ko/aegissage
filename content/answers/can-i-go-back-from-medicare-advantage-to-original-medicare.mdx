Yes. You can leave a Medicare Advantage plan and return to Original Medicare during Open Enrollment, October 15 to December 7, or during the Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, January 1 to March 31. The part worth planning ahead is getting a Medigap policy afterward, and your first year in Medicare Advantage has extra protection for exactly that.

## When can I switch back?

- **Open Enrollment, October 15 to December 7 each year.** The change starts January 1.
- **Medicare Advantage Open Enrollment Period, January 1 to March 31.** Only for people already in a Medicare Advantage plan. You can make one change, including going back to Original Medicare. It starts the first of the month after your request.
- **Special Enrollment Periods.** Some events, such as moving or your plan leaving Medicare, open a window at other times.

## What is the first-year trial right?

Medicare gives you a 12-month trial in two situations:

1. **You joined a Medicare Advantage plan when you were first eligible for Medicare at 65.** If you leave within the first year, you can buy any Medigap policy sold in your state, with no health questions.
2. **You dropped a Medigap policy to try Medicare Advantage for the first time.** If you leave within the first year, you can get your old policy back if the same company still sells it. If not, you can buy certain other Medigap plans.

Apply for the Medigap policy as soon as you decide, and no later than 63 days after your Medicare Advantage coverage ends.

## What if I have been in Medicare Advantage longer than a year?

You can still go back to Original Medicare during the enrollment periods. Whether a Medigap company must sell you a policy depends on where you live:

- **New Jersey:** outside your Medigap Open Enrollment Period and guaranteed issue situations, a company can generally ask health questions and may say no.
- **New York:** state law requires insurers to accept Medigap applications all year, regardless of health. See [Medigap underwriting in New Jersey and New York](/answers/medigap-underwriting-new-jersey-new-york).

If your plan leaves Medicare or stops serving your area, that also gives you a guaranteed right to buy certain Medigap plans.

## Don't forget drug coverage

Most Medicare Advantage plans include drug coverage. Original Medicare does not. When you switch back, join a Medicare drug plan at the same time so there is no gap.
