In New York City, Medicare Advantage and drug plans are offered county by county. Each of the five boroughs is its own county. Brooklyn is Kings County, and Queens is Queens County. New York State also gives residents two things most states do not. One is the right to buy a Medigap policy at any time. The other is a state prescription program called EPIC.

## Can I buy Medigap at any time in New York?

Yes. Federal law gives a one-time 6-month Medigap window, but New York goes further. State law lets anyone with Medicare Parts A and B buy a Medigap policy at any time, and insurers may not consider health, claims history or age.

According to the New York State Department of Financial Services, insurers must accept a Medigap application at any time of year.

New York Medigap premiums are also community-rated. They vary by where in the state you live and by company. They do not vary by your age or health.

This changes the decision for New Yorkers. In many states, choosing Medicare Advantage first and Medigap later can mean being turned down for Medigap. In New York that door stays open. It is still a decision about cost and networks, and the comparison guide [Medicare Advantage vs. Medigap](/medicare-basics/medicare-advantage-vs-medigap) covers the trade-offs.

## What is EPIC in New York?

EPIC is a New York State program that helps income-eligible residents 65 and older pay for prescriptions. It works together with a Medicare Part D drug plan to lower what you pay. You can apply at any time of year, and the state sets the income limits.

You can apply at any time of year, and you must be enrolled in, or eligible to enroll in, a Part D plan. Income limits and benefits are set by the state and listed on the Department of Health page in the sources below.

## Are Medicare enrollment dates different in New York?

No. The federal windows apply in New York. Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7 each year, and people already in a Medicare Advantage plan can switch once between January 1 and March 31. Plans themselves are offered borough by borough.

## Can I get Medicare help in my own language in New York?

Yes. 1-800-MEDICARE offers interpreters at no cost in any language. I also explain Medicare myself in English, Georgian and Russian, by phone or video. Family members are welcome to join the conversation, and there is no fee to talk with me.
