In New York City, Medicare Advantage and drug plans are offered county by county. Each of the five boroughs is its own county. Brooklyn is Kings County, and Queens is Queens County. New York State also gives residents two things most states do not. One is the right to buy a Medigap policy at any time. The other is a state prescription program called EPIC.

## Medigap: New York is different

Under federal law, the Medigap Open Enrollment Period lasts 6 months and happens once. New York goes further. According to the New York State Office for the Aging, state law continues that open enrollment. So a person enrolled in Medicare Parts A and B may buy a Medigap policy at any time. Insurers may not consider health status, claims experience or age.

New York Medigap premiums are also community-rated. They vary by where in the state you live and by company. They do not vary by your age or health.

This changes the decision for New Yorkers. In many states, choosing Medicare Advantage first and Medigap later can mean being turned down for Medigap. In New York that door stays open. It is still a decision about cost and networks, and the comparison guide [Medicare Advantage vs. Medigap](/medicare-basics/medicare-advantage-vs-medigap) covers the trade-offs.

## EPIC: help with prescription costs

EPIC is a New York State program for income-eligible residents 65 and older. It works together with a Medicare Part D drug plan to lower what you pay out of pocket. You can apply at any time of year, and you must be enrolled in, or eligible to enroll in, a Part D plan. Income limits and benefits are set by the state and listed on the Department of Health page in the sources below.

## Free counseling: HIICAP

New York's State Health Insurance Assistance Program is called HIICAP, the Health Insurance Information, Counseling and Assistance Program. Counselors explain Medicare, Medigap, EPIC and the Medicare Savings Programs, and they do not sell insurance. The statewide help line is 1-800-701-0501.

## Enrollment dates are the same as everywhere

The federal windows apply in New York. Open Enrollment runs October 15 to December 7. People already in a Medicare Advantage plan can switch once between January 1 and March 31. See [enrollment periods explained](/answers/medicare-enrollment-periods-explained).
