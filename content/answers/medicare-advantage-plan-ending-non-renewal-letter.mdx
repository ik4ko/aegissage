If you received a letter saying your Medicare Advantage plan will not be offered next year, you keep Medicare, and you have extra time to choose new coverage. Medicare gives people in a non-renewing plan a Special Enrollment Period from December 8 through the last day of February. To have new coverage start on January 1, make your choice by December 31.

## First, confirm what the letter says

Three different things get described as a plan "ending":

- **Non-renewal.** The plan's contract with Medicare is not renewed for next year. This is what the Special Enrollment Period above is for.
- **The plan is leaving your county.** The plan continues elsewhere but no longer serves your area.
- **A merger.** Your plan is being combined into another plan from the same company. In that case you are generally moved into the surviving plan and your choices are the ordinary enrollment periods.

Your notice says which one applies. If it is unclear, check next year's plans for your ZIP code at medicare.gov or call 1-800-MEDICARE (TTY 1-877-486-2048).

## Your options

1. **Join another Medicare Advantage plan** available in your county.
2. **Return to Original Medicare** and add a Part D drug plan. If you do nothing, this is where you land when your plan ends, and you may be without drug coverage until you join a drug plan.
3. **Return to Original Medicare and buy a Medigap policy.** When a Medicare Advantage plan leaves Medicare or stops serving your area, federal rules give you a guaranteed right to buy certain Medigap policies if you switch to Original Medicare. You can apply as early as 60 days before your coverage ends and no later than 63 days after.

## Dates to keep in front of you

- **October 15 to December 7:** Open Enrollment. A choice made here starts January 1.
- **December 8 to the last day of February:** the Special Enrollment Period for a non-renewing plan.
- **63 days after your plan coverage ends:** the last day to apply for a Medigap policy under the guaranteed issue right.

## Before you pick anything

Write down your doctors, your prescriptions and your pharmacy. A replacement plan that looks similar can have a different network and a different drug list. The [appointment checklist](/medicare-checklist) is a printable way to gather this, and [enrollment periods explained](/answers/medicare-enrollment-periods-explained) covers the other windows.

NJ SHIP counselors (1-800-792-8820) help with this at no cost and do not sell insurance.
