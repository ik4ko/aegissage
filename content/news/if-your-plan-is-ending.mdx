Some Medicare Advantage and drug plans are not renewing for next year, and
their members get a letter in the fall. If you got one, take a breath: you have
options and you have time.

You can choose new coverage during Open Enrollment, through December 7, so it
starts January 1. You also get a <G k="sep" /> that runs from December 8 to the
last day of February.
([Medicare.gov: Special Enrollment Periods](https://www.medicare.gov/basics/get-started-with-medicare/get-more-coverage/joining-a-plan/special-enrollment-periods))

If you do not join another Medicare Advantage plan before your plan ends, you
are enrolled in Original Medicare, so you are not left with nothing. A choice
made after December 7 generally starts the first of the month after the new
plan gets your request.

<Callout type="tip" title="Before you pick anything">
Write down your doctors and your prescriptions first. They are the two things
most likely to change when you move to a new plan, and the easiest to check
before you decide rather than after.
</Callout>

The longer answer, including what happens to a Medigap right when a plan ends,
is on [My Medicare Advantage plan is ending](/answers/medicare-advantage-plan-ending-non-renewal-letter).
