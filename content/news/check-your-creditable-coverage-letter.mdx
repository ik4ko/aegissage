If you are working past 65 and keeping your employer's health plan, there is
one piece of paper worth finding today.

Your plan is required to tell you each year, in writing, whether its drug
coverage counts as <G k="creditable-coverage" />. It usually arrives in the
fall and it usually gets thrown out.

Keep it. Going 63 days or more without creditable drug coverage starts a
<G k="part-d" /> late enrollment penalty that is permanent once it begins, and
that letter is how you prove you had coverage all along.

If you cannot find it, email HR and ask. It is a two-minute request and it is
the highest-value thing you can do this month.
