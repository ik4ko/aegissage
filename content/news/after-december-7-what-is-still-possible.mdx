People often worry that missing December 7 locks them in for a full year. For
many people, it does not.

If you are in a Medicare Advantage plan on January 1, the <G k="ma-oep" /> runs
January 1 to March 31. During it you can make one change: switch to another
Medicare Advantage plan, or leave Medicare Advantage and go back to Original
Medicare, with a separate drug plan if you want one. A change starts the first
of the month after the plan gets your request.

This window does not help someone who has Original Medicare and wants to move
into Medicare Advantage. That move waits for next fall's Open Enrollment,
unless a life event gives you a <G k="sep" />, such as moving, losing other
coverage, or getting help from Medicaid or Extra Help.

The [enrollment calendar](/medicare-enrollment-calendar) lists every window,
who it is for, and the official Medicare.gov page each one comes from. If you
are not sure which one fits you, ask me. I will tell you plainly, even if the
answer is "wait."
