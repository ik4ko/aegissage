Medicare <G k="aep" /> runs October 15 to December 7. Your plan has to get
your request by December 7, and any change you make starts January 1.
([Medicare.gov: Open Enrollment](https://www.medicare.gov/health-drug-plans/open-enrollment))

You do not have to change anything. Many people keep the plan they have, and
that is often the right call. The point of the check is to know rather than
assume.

<Callout type="tip" title="The ten-minute version">
Find your plan's <G k="anoc" /> letter for next year. Check two things: is every
doctor you see still in the plan's <G k="network" />, and is every prescription
you take still on the <G k="formulary" />, at the same tier? If both answers are
yes, you are likely done for the year.
</Callout>

Not sure how to check a doctor? [Here is how to check if a doctor is in a Medicare plan](/answers/how-to-check-if-a-doctor-is-in-a-medicare-plan).

If either answer is no, or you cannot find the letter, that is a good reason
to look at your options before December 7. You can do it yourself on
Medicare.gov, or call or text me and we can check your doctors and
prescriptions together. There is no cost to you and no obligation to enroll.
