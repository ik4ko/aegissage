---
title: "Myth of the week: Medicare pays for the nursing home"
description: "It is the most common and most expensive misunderstanding in retirement planning. Medicare covers skilled care, briefly, under conditions."
date: "2026-07-14"
category: "Myth of the week"
---

It does not. This one costs families more than any other Medicare
misunderstanding, and it is worth being blunt about.

Medicare covers **skilled** nursing care — the kind you need after a qualifying
hospital stay, for a limited number of days, while you are actively getting
better. It is rehabilitation, not residence.

What it does not cover is **custodial** care: help with bathing, dressing,
eating, getting around. Ongoing daily assistance is not a Medicare benefit no
matter how much you need it or how long you have paid in.

That gap is what Medicaid and long-term care insurance exist to fill. Which one
applies to a given family is a real planning conversation, and it is one worth
having before it is urgent rather than during a hospital discharge.
