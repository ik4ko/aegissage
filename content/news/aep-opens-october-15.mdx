The <G k="aep" /> runs October 15 through December 7. Anything you change takes
effect January 1.

You do not need to do anything on October 15. What you do need is to have read
your Annual Notice of Change before then — it arrives in September, it looks
like junk mail, and it tells you exactly what your plan is changing for next
year.

<Callout type="tip" title="The ten-minute version">
When that envelope shows up, check two things: is every prescription you take
still covered at the same tier, and is every doctor you see still in network.
If both are unchanged, you are done for the year.
</Callout>

If either answer is no, that is the year to look at your options — and the
window to do it closes December 7.
