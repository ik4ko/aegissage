# Partner program: compliance guardrails

**Status: draft for Pinnacle review. I am not a lawyer. These are the rules
as I understand them, written so you can check each one with Pinnacle
compliance before the partner kit is used. Where I am unsure, it says so.**

## 1. Educational events versus sales events (42 CFR 422.2264(c))

| | Educational event | Sales event |
|---|---|---|
| Purpose | Inform about Medicare in general | Steer toward a plan or set of plans |
| Advertised as | Educational | Sales |
| Plan names, premiums, benefits | No | Allowed |
| Enrollment forms | No | Allowed |
| Business cards | May be available; hand one over when asked | Yes |
| Sign-in sheets | If used, clearly optional | Clearly optional |

Working rules for every talk at a library, senior center, community or employer:
- Advertise it as educational and keep it that way. If the conversation turns to specific plans, say you can cover that separately and move on.
- No plan names, carrier names, premiums or benefit details in slides, handouts or answers.
- No enrollment forms in the room.
- An educational event needs no Scope of Appointment. At one, you may hand out materials, answer questions people ask, give out business cards, and make Scope of Appointment forms and reply cards available for people who want them (42 CFR 422.2264(c)(2)(ii)). You may not give a sales or marketing presentation or take applications there.
- A marketing event or one-on-one appointments may follow an educational event at the same place, the same day. The CY2027 final rule removed the 12-hour gap.
- Source: 42 CFR 422.2264(c), as amended by the CY2027 final rule (Federal Register 2026-06600, April 6 2026, in effect June 1 2026). Checked on eCFR Nov 17 2026.
- Meals are not allowed at sales events; light snacks are treated differently. Bring nothing and let the host decide.

## 2. Scope of Appointment (same day is fine)

- Before any one-to-one conversation about specific plans, the person agrees to the Scope of Appointment, the kinds of coverage you will discuss, and you record it. For an in-person appointment it must be in writing (42 CFR 422.2264(c)(3)(i)).
- There is no 48-hour wait. The CY2027 final rule removed it, so the SOA can be agreed and recorded and the appointment held the same day, including at a help day after a talk and when someone a partner mentioned you to calls in.
- Talk about only the kinds of coverage the person agreed to. A new topic needs a new SOA.
- All plan discussion and enrollment goes through the SOA and the recorded line. The recorded-line number is never printed on partner material.

## 3. Healthcare settings

- No sales activity where care is delivered: exam rooms, treatment areas, dialysis areas, hospital patient rooms, pharmacy counters.
- Common areas (cafeteria, community room, conference room, lobby) are treated differently.
- Providers and their staff may make plan-neutral materials available and may tell patients a broker exists. They should not recommend a plan, hand out one carrier's material only, or collect enrollment forms.
- In a long-term care facility, a visit to a resident's room happens only when the resident asks for it.
- Your former employer is never named or implied in any material.

## 4. Anti-Kickback Statute (42 U.S.C. 1320a-7b(b))

- It is a federal crime to offer, pay, ask for or receive anything of value to induce or reward referrals for items or services paid for by a federal health care program.
- The rule for this program is simple and has no exceptions: **nothing of value moves in either direction.** No referral fees, no per-lead payments, no gift cards, no sponsored lunches, no free services to a practice in return for introductions, no rent for table space above fair market value.
- If a partner asks for payment, sponsorship or a donation in connection with access to their people, decline and tell Pinnacle.
- This is the area where a lawyer's opinion matters most. If a partner arrangement goes beyond "they hand out my card", get advice before agreeing to it.

## 5. HIPAA

- A provider may not give you patient names, contact details or any health information for marketing without the patient's written authorization.
- So the program never involves lists. The person contacts you.
- Do not accept a list if one is offered. Do not sign a business associate agreement to get one; that changes your legal position and needs advice first.
- Your own forms never ask about health conditions, and never ask for a Medicare number, Social Security number or date of birth.

## 6. Plan neutrality

- Partner material names no insurance company and no plan.
- Every piece carries the TPMO disclaimer with the current count for the audience's county.
- No superlatives, no savings claims, no "free", no "$0", no statement that anyone qualifies for anything.
- No Medicare or government logo, and no wording that suggests Medicare sent you.
- A partner's name or logo is used only with their written permission, and never to suggest they endorse a plan.
