## Rules for every post

- **One link per post**, to the page named. Never the homepage alone.
- **Short, calm and plain**, for readers 64 and older and their adult children.
  - No "best", "top", "#1", "act now" or countdowns.
  - No "you qualify", and no promise of savings or a $0 premium.
  - No plan, carrier, hospital or doctor names. No prices, star ratings or benefit amounts.
  - "Free" only for things that really are free and are not a plan benefit: county SHIP counseling, a talk, a call with you.
- **No health questions** in posts or replies. If someone comments with their health details, reply: "Thanks, please call me so we can talk privately." Then call. Don't discuss it in public.
- **Town page links** (marked *after town PR*) only work once the town pages PR is merged. Until then, swap in the county page in brackets.
- **Sign-off** (optional, plain): `Erekle Niniashvili, licensed insurance broker · (551) 202-9079`
